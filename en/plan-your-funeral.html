Plan your funeral, while you are alive.

Relieve your loved ones of the cost of your funeral and have your wishes respected: that is what the Murith funeral plan contract provides.

The purpose of funeral pre-planning

A simple gesture, one less burden
for your family.

More and more people plan their own funeral in advance. The main aim: to free their loved ones financially, and to be certain their wishes will be respected.

Because those who remain already have enough to bear.

Your loved ones are freed from the financing

Your funeral is organised and paid for in advance. Your family does not have to advance any costs at a time when they should only be thinking of being together.

Your wishes, in black and white

Ceremony, music, arrangements: every wish is recorded and will be carried out.

A moment for you

Take your time, at your own pace, with an attentive advisor.

Talk to an advisor
Two ways to plan

A simple package, or special requests.

In both cases, your funeral advisors take care of everything and record every wish in your contract.

Simple package

A sober ceremony, organised along your broad lines, without particular arrangements. The essentials are settled; your loved ones have nothing to decide.

Special requests

Music, venue, order of the ceremony: your precise wishes are put in black and white and will be respected.
