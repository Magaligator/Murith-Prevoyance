Funeral planning: organise your funeral while you are alive.

By Magali Murith K.·Published 20 January 2023·Updated 14 October 2026·3 min read

Have you ever worried, or even thought, about how your funeral will be organised? Are you afraid your loved ones might face difficulties after you are gone? Do you already have an idea of how you would like your funeral to be arranged?

1. Planning your funeral in advance: have you thought about it?

Many people would like to take out a funeral plan. However, they face a major obstacle: the cost.

Not everyone can necessarily pay in cash, but with our payment options, our offer suits everyone. Indeed, most funeral plans require payment in full up front, without offering instalments.

Murith Prévoyance gives you peace of mind by organising your funeral while you are alive.

2. Funeral planning in Geneva, in 3 steps with no commitment

Funeral planning in Geneva is within everyone’s reach. With Murith Prévoyance, in 3 steps, you receive an offer with no commitment.

  1. Fill in our questionnaire to receive an offer.
  2. We get in touch for further information, if needed.
  3. You receive an offer for funeral planning as soon as possible.

Our funeral advisors are here to support you in organising your funeral in Geneva and to advise you on the funeral plan contract suited to your needs, so that everything takes place with complete peace of mind.

3. Organising your funeral in Geneva with respect for beliefs and cultures

At Murith Prévoyance, we make a point of respecting the beliefs, traditions and culture of every community. We work with all religious and civil bodies, with complete discretion. After a first contact, we coordinate all the arrangements for you, in Geneva as well as internationally.

5. A funeral planning service based in Geneva

With Murith Prévoyance, you no longer have to worry about your departure. You can leave with serenity. Your family will be relieved of the financial and administrative burden that a funeral can bring. We take care of the administrative formalities and the planning of the ceremony, right up to your last goodbye to your loved ones.

The idea of planning your own funeral can be frightening. Some still prefer to avoid thinking about it. Today, funeral planning needs to become commonplace, so as to anticipate the burden a funeral can place on your loved ones.

Once your funeral plan contract is finalised, you can have your personal account with your payment and contract information, to bring transparency to you and your family after you are gone.
