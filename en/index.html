Payment options

Two ways to pay, tailored to you.

In both cases, finalising your contract gives you access to your online area to view your information at any time.

Single payment

The payment covers all funeral services in one go.

  • Your funeral is paid in full
  • Contract card with your number, sent as soon as payment is received
  • Nothing for your loved ones to pay

Instalments

More flexible: spread the cost over several months.

  • Total amount of services guaranteed
  • Payment tracking account, on request
  • Contract card sent from the first payment
If death occurs before the end of the payment term, the balance is claimed from the estate.

Frequently asked questions

You may be wondering…

Another question? Call us on +41 22 809 56 00.

Why take out a funeral plan contract?
To decide your own funeral and pay for it in advance. Your loved ones neither have to make choices in a rush nor advance the costs.
What guarantees does Murith Prévoyance offer?
Your contract sets out the services you have chosen, and the total amount of services is guaranteed. The details of your guarantees are given to you with your quote.
How does my family access the arrangements?
You receive a card bearing your contract number. Your loved ones present it to Murith, who then carries out the wishes recorded in your contract.
